Why photos are “must answer”, not “must agree”

If a child could only keep their place by letting us publish their photo, the yes wouldn’t really be a choice. So a guardian has to answer, and either answer keeps the place. A “no” stays on the child’s record, and the media team’s photo tools hide that child.

Every answer goes into a log that is never overwritten. The latest answer counts, and the full history stays on file.

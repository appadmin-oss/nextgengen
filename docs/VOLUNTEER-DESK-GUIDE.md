# Volunteer Desk Guide

You check people in at the door. Scan the card, check that the name and photo
match the person, then do what the screen tells you. The same steps work at the
NGG gate desk and the CACENTRE gate desk. Each desk also carries this guide on
screen: **Volunteer guide** under the camera (NGG), or the **Guide** button at
the top (CACENTRE).

## Before you start

You need a phone or tablet with a camera, charged, and the desk link the
coordinator sends you.

| | NGG gate desk | CACENTRE gate desk |
|---|---|---|
| Link looks like | `…/gate?g=…` | `cacentre.afrovanguard.org.ng/desk/…` |
| To open it | Type the **desk password** and press **Unlock the desk** — it opens straight onto check-in | Type the **desk password**, press **Open the desk** |
| Who it takes | Summer School participants, volunteers, NGG members | CACENTRE staff and visitors, NGG cards, Afrovanguard members |

The desk password is given to you in person. Never write it on the phone, on
paper near the desk, or in a group chat. Allow the camera when the browser asks.

## Every arrival

1. On the CACENTRE desk, check that **Check in** is gold at the top. Blue means **Check out**.
2. Hold the card's QR code 15–20 cm from the camera, flat and still. A card reader plugged in, or a card tapped on the back of an Android phone, works too.
3. Read the big word, then look at the name and photo. **They must match the person in front of you.** If they don't, do not let them in; call the coordinator.
4. Do what the screen tells you, then call the next person.

Scanning the same card twice is safe. A day counts once.

## What the screen says

A green result fades by itself. **Do not admit** stays on screen until you press
**OK — next person**.

| You see | What it means | Do this |
|---|---|---|
| **In** / **Checked in** | Recorded | Let them in. |
| **In — late** / **Late** | Recorded as late | Let them in. Say nothing about fines. |
| **Already in today** | Scanned earlier today | Let them in. |
| **Checked out** | Departure recorded | Goodbye. CACENTRE desk, check-out mode only. |
| **Saved on this phone** / **Saved offline** | The internet dropped; the scan is waiting | Let them in. Keep the page open; it sends by itself. |
| **Do not admit** | Suspended, evicted, membership not active, or pass withdrawn | Do not let them in. Do not argue. Call the coordinator. |
| **Not marked** / **Not accepted** | The card did not work | Read the box under it. Usually: find them by name, or type their code. |

## When something is off

| Problem | Do this |
|---|---|
| No card, or it won't scan | Type the code printed under the QR. An NGG member can also give their ID, e.g. `026-014`. On the CACENTRE desk you can open **No card? Find them by name**. |
| **Card expired** (CACENTRE desk) | Find them by name to let them in today. Tell them to ask the office for a new card. |
| **ID expired** or **season ended** (NGG desk) | Do not let them in on it. Send them to the coordinator. |
| **Card replaced** | They have an old member card. Ask for the new one, or type their member ID. |
| **Pass expired** (Afrovanguard) | Ask them to open the pass again on their phone, then scan the new one. |
| A visitor (CACENTRE desk) | Open **A visitor**. Fill in their name and who they are seeing. Give them a blank card if you have one. |
| Leaving early | CACENTRE desk: switch to **Check out**, scan, then switch back. NGG desk: the coordinator records it. |
| "Waiting to send" or "saved offline" | The phone is holding scans. Keep the page open until the message goes. |
| Camera black | Press **Try the camera again**, or allow the camera in the browser's settings. Meanwhile, type codes. |
| Safeguarding warning on a volunteer | They must not be left alone with young people. Tell the coordinator. |

## End of your shift

- [ ] No "waiting to send" or "saved offline" message is showing.
- [ ] If you are on attendance duty at the NGG desk, press **Close the roll**.
- [ ] On the CACENTRE desk, press **Lock** at the top.
- [ ] Hand the phone to the next volunteer or the coordinator. Do not share the password by message.

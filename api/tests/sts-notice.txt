STREET TO STARDOM (STS)

 *MANAGEMENT OFFICIAL NOTICE* 

SUBJECT: *REVISED SATURDAY RESUMPTION, ATTENDANCE, DISCIPLINE AND HOUSEHOLD RESPONSIBILITY POLICY* 

Following constructive input from concerned parents and guardians, STS Management, in collaboration with its volunteers, has approved reasonable adjustments to Saturday activities.

This decision is informed by the need to improve the quality of responsibility among our children at home, particularly our male children, who must be deliberately encouraged to participate actively in household chores and family responsibilities.

We believe that raising responsible children goes beyond academic excellence. Every child must learn to contribute meaningfully to the home, develop practical life skills and demonstrate accountability.

1. *SATURDAY RESUMPTION TIME* 

- All Saturday activities now commence at 9:00 AM.
- Any arrival after 9:30 AM will officially be recorded as lateness.

2. *LATENESS AND DISCIPLINARY MEASURES* 

The following penalties apply:

- 9:30 AM to before 9:45 AM: ₦500 fine and 3 frog jumps.
- 9:45 AM to before 10:00 AM: 5 frog jumps.
- Arrival at or after 10:00 AM will require management review and appropriate disciplinary action.

3. *ABSENCE AND ATTENDANCE POLICY* 

- Absence without prior notice or approved permission attracts a ₦1,000 fine and 7 frog jumps.
- Three absences within one session will result in suspension.
- A suspended child may resume after one week upon payment of a ₦5,000 re-entry fee.
- Only five absences with permission may be accepted within a session.
- Approved absences must not interfere with the submission of assignments, projects and other assigned responsibilities.

4. *REQUESTING PERMISSION* 

- All permission requests must be submitted through the official NGG Members Dashboard. next.afrovanguard.org.ng
- Permission requests will no longer be accepted through physical letters or informal messages.
- The only exception is an absence extending beyond three consecutive weeks, for which a formal written application will be required.
- Submitting a request does not automatically constitute approval. Parents and guardians must ensure that permission is approved through the appropriate channel.

5. *ASSIGNMENT AND PROJECT ACCOUNTABILITY* 

Every child is expected to take personal responsibility for assigned tasks and projects.

- Children must take screenshots or photographic evidence of completed assignments and upload them to the STS Members Dashboard.
- Failure to submit the required evidence will attract a ₦1,000 fine.
- Parents and guardians are encouraged to monitor their children's progress and ensure timely submission.

6. *FRESH START: REVOCATION OF PREVIOUS OFFENCES* 

All offences and pending disciplinary penalties recorded before this announcement are hereby revoked.

Every child starts afresh under this revised policy. The new rules take immediate effect.

 *OUR COLLECTIVE RESPONSIBILITY* 

We appreciate the concerned parents and guardians who have contributed to improving our approach to raising responsible children.

STS is not merely an academic or extracurricular programme. It is a character-building platform where discipline, accountability, diligence, responsibility and integrity must become part of everyday life.

We are raising together a generation that will not be *bought, bent or broken.* 

Together, we are raising an *INCORRUPTIBLE GENERATION!*

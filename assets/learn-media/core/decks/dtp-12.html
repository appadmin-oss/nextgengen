Desktop Publishing · Week 12 · Idea 1

Understand it

Your magazine is the term's work in one publication. You need:

  • a flat plan: a small drawing of all four pages showing what goes where;
  • a cover with masthead, a strong image and two cover lines;
  • three inside pages on one grid, with consistent styles, at least one story, one photo story and one data graphic (link to Data Appreciation!);
  • legal images, credited, with consent for people;
  • a print-ready PDF with bleed;
  • a three-minute presentation: your choices and why.
Desktop Publishing · Week 12 · Idea 2

Understand it

Go back to your earlier work before you start: the week 4 hierarchy, the week 5 grid and the week 10 bleed set-up are what this magazine is built from, so use them again.

The panel will ask about YOUR decisions — the grid, the hierarchy, the colours. That is how they know the work is yours.

Desktop Publishing · Week 12 · Practice

Now you do it

  1. Plan the four pages as a flat plan
  2. Write, photograph and design your pages
  3. Pre-flight, export and present to the panel

Need a hand? Use a Canva magazine template as the starting grid; replace every element.

Done early? Produce a printed copy and a screen version with alt text for every image.

Desktop Publishing · Week 12 · Assessment

How you will be assessed

  • Your four-page magazine: upload the print-ready PDF and your flat plan, and describe your design decisions.
  • Name one design decision you made and why it helps the reader.

Your work is marked against the rubric, and you may be asked to explain your choices. Show your own thinking.

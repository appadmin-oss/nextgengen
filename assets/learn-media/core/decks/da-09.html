Data Appreciation · Week 9 · Idea 1

Understand it

Better searching:

  • "quotes" find an exact phrase: "Lagos population".
  • site: searches one site: site:nigerianstat.gov.ng inflation.
  • -word removes results: jaguar -car.
  • Add the year for recent facts: 2026.

Before you believe or share, use SIFT:

  1. Stop — do you know this site or person?
  2. Investigate the source — who made it, when, why?
  3. Find better coverage — what do trusted sources say?
  4. Trace claims back to the original data or quote.
Data Appreciation · Week 9 · Idea 2

Understand it

A screenshot is not a source. A number with no source, date or link is a claim, not a fact.

AI chatbots answer fluently and are sometimes wrong, with invented sources. Use an answer as a list of things to check, and check them.

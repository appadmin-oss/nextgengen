Data Appreciation · Week 4 · Idea 1

Understand it

Ten shortcuts every user should know (on a Mac, Cmd instead of Ctrl):

  • Ctrl+C copy · Ctrl+X cut · Ctrl+V paste
  • Ctrl+Z undo · Ctrl+Y redo
  • Ctrl+S save · Ctrl+F find
  • Ctrl+A select all · Ctrl+P print · Alt+Tab switch window

Typing: eyes on the screen, fingers home on ASDF JKL;, and accuracy first — fixing errors costs more than typing slowly.

Entering data is where most mistakes start. A list where one person wrote 14/3/26, another March 14 and a third 14-03-2026 cannot be sorted. Agree a format before you start and keep it: one date format, the same spelling every time (Egbeda, never egbeda or Egbedda), numbers with no units typed in (900, with ₦ in the heading). Then check: read it back, or have a partner read it.
